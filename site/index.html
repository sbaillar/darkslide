01 / Features

Built for the way you shoot.

Card import, culling, sessions, editing, export and archiving. The core is Rust and renders on the GPU through Metal. The app is native SwiftUI.

1/250

Instant browsing

Thumbnails come from the embedded preview straight away. Next photo in under 50 ms.

F2.8

Smooth editing

The whole develop pipeline runs as GPU compute. Dragging a slider re-renders in 16 ms or less.

ISO 400

Sessions, not a catalog

Each session is a self-contained folder on any drive. Sidecars are the truth; the index is rebuildable.

AF-C

Your files are never lost

Atomic writes, BLAKE3-verified imports, nothing deleted from a card or archive, moved files found by hash.

AI

On-device AI

Culling, denoise, upscale, masks and search all run on your Mac. Nothing leaves it unless you ask.

⌘K

Edit with words

Type “warmer, a bit brighter” and only what you named changes. Unclear words are reported, never guessed.

C1

Reads Capture One

Import ratings, tags, keywords and supported adjustments, with a dry run first and a way to revert.

RAW

Cull by voice

Say “pick”, “reject” or “three stars”. Speech is recognised on device.

03 / What's new

Shipping now, and what is next.

“Available now” is on the main branch today. “Coming soon” is being built and reviewed and may change before it lands.

Available now

  • Capture One–style tool tabsTabs across the top of the tools pane; the + button adds or removes any tool in one click.
  • Import Capture One settingsExport recipes, the tool workspace, crop ratios, styles and keyword libraries, with a dry run and a revert.
  • Mac ↔ iPad link, Mac sideA secure, paired link over your LAN or Tailscale, set up with a QR code. Off by default. The iPad app itself is not built yet.
  • Reproducible benchmark kitMeasure Darkslide against other apps on your own machine and photos.
  • Single-instance guardOpening Darkslide twice no longer starts a second copy on the same library.
  • Richer voice commandsCounts, positions and several-photo commands such as “reject the next 3” or “go to photo 12”, recognised on device.

Coming soon

  • Cross-session LibraryA fast library across all sessions; opens in 44 ms with 100,000 photos in our benchmark.
  • Plug-insLooks, styles, voice packs and sandboxed WebAssembly export steps.
  • Google Photos exportSend finished photos to Google Photos.
  • Flexible filmstrip and layoutsA resizable multi-row filmstrip at the bottom, left or right, and multi-photo viewers (two side by side, 2×2 and more).
  • AI Changes listSee what the AI changed, with yellow highlights and selective undo.
  • Wider camera supportCanon CR2/CR3, Nikon, Fujifilm, Olympus/OM, Panasonic, Pentax, Leica, DNG and ProRAW.
  • Lightroom Classic importBring in catalogs and XMP.

Build from source

Signed, ready-to-run downloads are not available yet. You need macOS 26 on Apple silicon, the Xcode Command Line Tools (Swift 6.2) and Rust 1.90 or newer. Nothing in the build needs a secret, a certificate, a model or sample photos.

# build dist/Darkslide.app and run its self-test
DS_SKIP_MODELS=1 scripts/build-app.sh --test
# build and launch
DS_SKIP_MODELS=1 scripts/build-app.sh --run
# synthetic photos; touches none of your files
dist/Darkslide.app/Contents/MacOS/Darkslide --demo

07 / FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is it free?

Yes. Darkslide is open source under the Apache License 2.0.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. The reference machine is an M4 Pro.

Does my data leave my Mac?

No, unless you ask. The AI features run on device. The things that touch a network are ones you set up: archiving to a NAS, the Amazon Photos hand-off, exports you send somewhere, and the optional Mac ↔ iPad link, which is off by default. The optional AI Upscale model (about 34 MB) is downloaded the first time you use it, after you agree to its terms; nothing is fetched otherwise. The map asks once before requesting tiles from Apple Maps, and you can turn that off in Settings. Your photos and their locations are never uploaded.

Will it change my original files?

Never. Edits live in sidecars, not in the RAW file. Imports are verified by checksum before they count as done, and nothing is ever deleted from a memory card or from the archive.

Can I move over from Capture One or Lightroom?

From Capture One, yes: Darkslide reads a Capture One session (read-only, with a dry run first) and imports ratings, colour tags, keywords, picks and supported adjustments. It can also import Capture One settings such as export recipes, workspace, crop ratios and styles. Lightroom Classic catalog and XMP import is coming soon, not available yet.

Which cameras are supported?

The reference camera is the Sony A9 (ILCE-9), and most cameras supported by rawler should work. Wider, tested support (Canon, Nikon, Fujifilm, Olympus/OM, Panasonic, Pentax, Leica, DNG and ProRAW) is coming soon.

Is there an iPad app?

Not yet; it is planned. The Mac side of the secure Mac ↔ iPad link is available, and the ds-remote command-line client exercises it today.

How do I write a plug-in?

Plug-ins are coming soon, with a guide on writing them. Until then there is nothing to write against.

How do I report a bug or a security issue?

Open an issue on GitHub for bugs. For security problems, follow SECURITY.md instead of posting in public.

Is it affiliated with Adobe or Capture One?

No. Darkslide is not affiliated with or endorsed by Adobe or Capture One A/S. Their names are trademarks of their owners.
